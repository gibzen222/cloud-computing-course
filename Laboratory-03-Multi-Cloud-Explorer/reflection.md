# Mission Reflection

Completing this cloud computing mission helped me understand how different cloud platforms can support different business needs. The cloud platform that impressed me the most was **AWS** because it offers many services for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other business requirements. I also learned that AWS can support both small startups and large enterprises through its wide range of cloud services.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide services for virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and Kubernetes. They also offer scalable and flexible cloud infrastructure, allowing businesses to increase or decrease resources depending on their needs. Although their services have different names and features, they generally provide solutions for similar types of workloads.

Business requirements greatly influence cloud platform selection. A company needs to consider its budget, existing technology, security requirements, performance, scalability, and the type of application it wants to run. For example, a company using many Microsoft products may benefit from Azure, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence may consider GCP.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned to analyze a client's situation and match its requirements with the appropriate cloud services. This helped me develop better decision-making and problem-solving skills.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added organized documentation, cloud service comparisons, client recommendations, and Linux investigation results. The portfolio now demonstrates not only my understanding of cloud computing concepts but also my ability to document technical work and make practical cloud recommendations.
